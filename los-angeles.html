Why clean your solar panels?

Southern California sun is a gift — but LA air and rooftop debris can leave a film that blocks light. Regular, gentle cleaning helps panels stay efficient and looking well maintained.

  • More of the sun you paid for

    Dirt and residue can cut light reaching the cells. A clean surface helps your system generate closer to its potential.

  • Safe, careful methods

    We clean with methods suited to solar glass — no harsh shortcuts that risk scratches or warranty concerns.

  • Homes and commercial sites

    From residential rooftops to small business and commercial arrays — tell us about your property when you enquire.

  • Built for LA conditions

    Dry spells, wildfire ash seasons, and urban dust mean panels here often need more than rain alone can manage.
