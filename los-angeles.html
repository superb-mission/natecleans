Solar panel cleaning FAQ

Quick answers for Los Angeles homeowners and businesses considering solar cleaning.

How often should solar panels be cleaned in LA?
Many LA systems benefit from cleaning once or twice a year, and sooner after heavy dust, pollen, or wildfire ash. Your roof exposure and nearby trees matter — tell us your situation when you enquire and we can advise.
Isn’t rain enough to clean solar panels?
Rain helps with loose dust, but LA’s long dry spells and sticky film from smog, bird droppings, and ash often need a proper soft wash. Rain alone rarely restores a fully clean surface.
Is solar panel cleaning safe for my panels?
We use panel-safe soft-wash methods and work carefully around mounts and wiring. We don’t use abrasive pads or harsh shortcuts. We won’t claim manufacturer warranty outcomes we can’t guarantee — careful cleaning is the goal.
Do you clean residential and commercial solar?
Yes. Residential rooftop and ground-mount arrays, plus commercial and small-business rooftop solar across Greater LA. Include panel count and roof type in your quote request so we can price accurately.
